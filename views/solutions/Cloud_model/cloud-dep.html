Cloud Deployment Models

There is no single cloud model that fits every organization. Business requirements, security policies, compliance obligations, workload characteristics, and operational objectives determine the most suitable deployment strategy.

Modern enterprises typically adopt Private Cloud, Public Cloud, or Hybrid Cloud architectures depending on their operational and regulatory requirements.

Private Cloud Advantages

  • Complete infrastructure ownership and control.
  • Enhanced security and compliance management.
  • Predictable performance.
  • No dependency on public cloud providers.
  • Ideal for sensitive workloads and regulated industries.

Public Cloud Advantages

  • Rapid deployment of applications and services.
  • Virtually unlimited scalability.
  • Lower upfront investment.
  • Global infrastructure availability.
  • Consumption-based pricing models.

Hybrid Cloud Advantages

  • Combines security of private cloud with scalability of public cloud.
  • Supports workload portability.
  • Enables cloud bursting during peak demand.
  • Improves business continuity and disaster recovery.
  • Optimizes infrastructure investments.
