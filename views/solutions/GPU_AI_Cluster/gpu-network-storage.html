High-Speed Network Fabric

In a GPU cluster, networking is just as important as the GPUs themselves.

Modern AI workloads often run across multiple GPU nodes. During training, GPUs constantly exchange gradients, model parameters, and synchronization data. If the network cannot keep up, expensive GPUs spend time waiting instead of processing data.

The Hidden Problem

Many organizations invest heavily in GPUs but underestimate networking requirements. As a result, GPU utilization drops while training times increase significantly.

Choosing the Right AI Network

Different AI environments require different networking architectures.

Interconnect Comparison Matrix

Technology Typical Speed Best For
25G Ethernet 25 Gbps Small AI Labs
100G Ethernet 100 Gbps Enterprise AI
RoCE (RDMA over Converged Ethernet) 100-400 Gbps Large AI Platforms
InfiniBand 200-800 Gbps AI Factories

Industry Reality

Small AI environments often use Ethernet successfully. Large AI training environments typically adopt RoCE or InfiniBand to minimize communication overhead between GPU nodes.

Storage Design Principles

Storage is one of the most overlooked components in AI infrastructure design.

AI models consume enormous volumes of training data. If storage systems cannot deliver data fast enough, GPU utilization drops immediately.
