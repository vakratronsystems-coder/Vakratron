Illustrative scenario

An HR policy assistant for 3,000 employees

How we would build an assistant that answers HR and policy questions in English and Hindi, from current documents only, without exposing HR-only material.

This is a composite example built from common enterprise requirements. It is not a specific client engagement, and the figures are design targets for the scenario, not measured results.

Users~3,000 employees
LanguagesEnglish and Hindi
SourcesSharePoint and HR system
RuleAnswer with sources, or say so
The situation

The same questions, every day

The HR team answered hundreds of emails a month about leave, reimbursements, travel rules and benefits. Answers existed in policy documents, but there were several versions of many of them, some scanned, and some HR-only documents sat in the same folders as staff policies.

The approach

What we would do

  1. Collect 150 real questions from the HR mailbox, with the right answers marked by the HR team.
  2. Clean up sources with HR. Archive old versions, re-scan unreadable PDFs, and separate HR-only documents.
  3. Carry permissions into the index from Active Directory groups, so HR-only content is invisible to other staff.
  4. Hybrid search with reranking and a multilingual embedding model for Hindi questions.
  5. Answer only with sources. If nothing relevant is found, say so and point to the HR helpdesk.
  6. Pilot with two departments, then roll out company-wide.

Trade-offs

What we would flag

  • Document owners are essential. When a policy changes, someone must update the source, or the assistant repeats the old rule.
  • Some questions need a person. Personal cases and exceptions should be routed to HR, not answered by the assistant.
  • Hindi quality needs its own testing. Include enough Hindi questions in the test set.
