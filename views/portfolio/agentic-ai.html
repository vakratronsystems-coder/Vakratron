AI agents that do real work, within clear limits

An AI agent does not just answer. It plans steps, uses tools such as your ticketing, directory or ERP systems, and checks its own results. That makes it useful, and it also makes limits essential: what it may read, what it may change, and what a person must approve.

Where agents work today

Good first candidates

  • IT and HR service desks: triage, diagnosis, and routine fixes such as unlocks and certificate renewals.
  • Document-heavy checks: comparing invoices with purchase orders, or tender documents with requirements.
  • Operations support: gathering logs and metrics during an incident and drafting the summary for engineers.
  • Internal requests: collecting details, checking policy and raising a correctly filled request for approval.

Good candidates are frequent, rule-based, involve several systems, and have a clear point where a person can approve.
